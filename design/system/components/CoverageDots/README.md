# CoverageDots

Seven dots for the days of a Monday to Sunday week: filled where there is data, outlined where it is missing, absent for days still to come.

## Use
- Beside every weekly aggregate, followed by the words ("5 of 7 days"). The dots are for the eye; the words are for VoiceOver and for exactness.
- Filled `ink`, missing as a `track` ring, future days not drawn (so Wednesday shows three dots, not seven).
- HRV and other sample-count metrics use the words only ("12 samples").
